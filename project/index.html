What is AutoSol Africa?

AutoSol Africa is a next-generation digital vehicle health platform designed to bridge the gap between drivers and complex automotive technology. By simplifying data retrieved from your vehicle's onboard computers, we give you real-time insight into engine performance, breakdown prevention, and fault code interpretations before you even step into a mechanical workshop.

How OBD-II Works

Every modern vehicle features an onboard computer system known as OBD-II (On-Board Diagnostics). This system constantly monitors engine performance, emissions levels, and electrical sensors throughout your car.

When a sensor detects an operational issue, it logs a 5-character Diagnostic Trouble Code (DTC) and illuminates your Check Engine Light.
