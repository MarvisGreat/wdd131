Vulnerability Severity Matrix

The operational logic tier rules prioritize diagnostic alerts based on immediate mechanical risk indicators:

Severity Level Indicator Light State Risk Assessment Recommended Action
Low Solid Orange (Stored Code) Minor efficiency or emissions variance. Schedule general maintenance testing at convenience.
Moderate Solid Orange (Active Code) System component failure active. Delayed fixes risk long-term decay. Inspect system sensors within 48 operational hours.
High Flashing Check Engine Light Critical operational failure. Imminent powertrain damage risks. Pull over safely and pull terminal diagnostic logs immediately.
